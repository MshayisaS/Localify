Turn Your Small Shop Into a Digital Giant.

Localify helps local shops upload their inventory, share product images, and appear on every digital touchpoint—so you can compete with big stores and get found by more customers.


The Problem We Solve (Social Proof / Pain Point)

Your shop is full of great products. But if customers can’t see them online… do they exist?

Big retailers win because shoppers can browse their inventory 24/7. Local shops lose foot traffic simply because people don’t know what’s in stock.

Localify fixes that. No website builder. No tech headaches. Just upload, show, and sell.


How it Works

Step 1: Sign Up
Create your free Localify account. Enter your shop name, address, and category.

Step 2: Upload Your Catalogue
Take photos of your products or scan your existing price list. Upload images, add prices, and show your inventory.

Step 3: Go Live
We distribute your shop and products across digital touchpoints. Customers find you. Foot traffic grows.
