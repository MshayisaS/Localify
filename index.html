About Us

Localify: Empowering South African Entrepreneurs, One Click at a Time.

Established in 2026, Localify was born from a simple yet powerful belief: the heart of South Africa’s economy beats in its local businesses. We are a web-based service dedicated to leveling the playing field, giving local shop owners the tools they need to stand toe-to-toe with big retail chains. We provide a comprehensive digital platform that allows businesses to showcase their full inventory and enable customers to place orders online with ease. More than just a software solution, Localify is an upliftment initiative. We are committed to empowering South African owners—welcoming every registered, locally-owned shop. For those not yet registered, we offer hands-on assistance to get them up and running at a nominal cost, ensuring that no local entrepreneur is left behind in the digital economy.
