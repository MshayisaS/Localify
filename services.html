Our Services

Localify offers two tiers which is premium and basic, each tier has unique benefits and is structured for particular businesses. Customers are advised to be honest with themselves and their business status and choose what is appealing to them.


Basic

Our Basic plan, offers a range of things such as onboarding to our platform, your store being listed as a trusted local shop and members of the community able to view your inventory, provided it is regularly updated.

Basic plan offers the following:

  • Set up social media platforms, once off
  • Shop images uploaded to our platforms, once off

Premium

Our premium plan, offers a range of services such as a complete digital presence whhich compromises of us handling all social media advertisigng for your shop, and we target local people to ensure that you get the best results. We also will upload your shops inventory monthly and also advise on how to do specials fo your shop and brand.

Premium comes with the following:

  • Social media handling
  • Inventory uplisting
  • images can be uploaded at anytime and information changed accordingly
  • Ability to let customers buy online
